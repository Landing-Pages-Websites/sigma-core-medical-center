### Learn About Treatment-Specific Risks

Risks vary by hormone, dose, delivery method, and individual health factors. Menopausal hormone therapy, testosterone therapy, and other options each involve different considerations. Not necessarily. Sleep issues, stress, medication effects, thyroid conditions, anemia, and other health concerns can cause similar symptoms. A qualified clinician can consider your history and decide whether hormone testing or another evaluation makes sense.

**Can I start hormone therapy based on one lab result?**  
A single result should not be considered in isolation. Ask how the result relates to your symptoms, medical history, medications, and other relevant information, and whether additional evaluation is needed.

**Are supplements a substitute for hormone treatment or evaluation?**
