Source: approved section frames; section manifest wins for content. CSS viewport used for review: 1440×900 laptop and 390×844 mobile. 
Design frames are 1536×864 bitmaps (medical-sources and some privacy/CTA bands are 1536×512). Their CSS viewport and DPR are unknown.
Use approved typography_contract roles h1/display, body and utility; preserve the approved palette_contract tokens ink, surface, action, electric, silver, charcoal and light. CSS owns fields, bars, brackets, beveled rails, labels, buttons and forms. Photography belongs to its section.
No medical outcomes, team identities, credentials, facility contact details, guide delivery or calendar behavior may be inferred from these visual frames.
Visual QA limitation: source frame photography is sometimes visibly clipped by approved geometric fields or other content; the crops preserve only source-visible subject pixels. Do not fabricate occluded parts, enlarge tiny photo strips beyond readable resolution, or present rendered source-frame rooms as verified client photographs.
